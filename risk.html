Risk disclosure

Last updated: August 2026 · Applies to the TradePro demo platform.

TradePro is a front-end demonstration. Market data is simulated locally in your browser, no real money can be deposited, withdrawn or traded, and no order is ever sent to a real exchange or liquidity provider.

1. General trading risk warning

In a live environment, contracts for difference (CFDs) and other leveraged instruments are complex products that carry a high risk of losing money rapidly due to leverage. The majority of retail investor accounts lose money when trading CFDs. You should consider whether you understand how these products work and whether you can afford to take the high risk of losing your money.

  • Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.
  • Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose.
  • Leverage magnifies both gains and losses.
  • Prices can move against your position faster than you can react.

2. Leverage and margin

This demo applies the following maximum leverage tiers by asset class:

  • Forex: up to 1:100
  • Commodities: up to 1:20
  • Indices: up to 1:20
  • Stocks: up to 1:5
  • Crypto: up to 1:2

When your margin level falls too low in a live brokerage, positions may be automatically liquidated at prevailing market prices — potentially at a loss you did not anticipate.

3. Execution and pricing

All prices shown on this demo are generated by a local random-walk simulation. They do not reflect real market prices, spreads or liquidity. In production environments, execution speeds and slippage can vary with market conditions.

4. Frequently asked questions

Is this a real brokerage?
No. TradePro is a UI/UX demonstration. Accounts, balances, orders and market data exist only inside your own browser via localStorage.
How do I log in?
Use the seeded demo account: demo@tradepro.com with password Demo1234!. Or create a new account from the register page — it stays entirely on this device.
Where is my data stored?
Everything (profile, balance, watchlist, positions) lives in your browser’s localStorage under the key tp_state_v1. Clearing site data resets everything. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
Why did my limit order fill instantly?
Pending limit and stop orders are checked against the simulated feed every second. If price touches your trigger level, the fill is immediate — just like a fast-moving real market.
Can I use this as a template for a real product?
The layout and components are yours to reuse, but any real deployment requires a licensed broker backend, KYC/AML flows, regulatory approval and a proper security audit.
Does it cost anything?
No. It is a free front-end demo with no ads, no tracking and no server calls except CDN-hosted fonts, icons and the charting library.
